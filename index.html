01 / ABOUT

Built for systems where downtime isn't an option

I'm a frontend engineer with 10 years of experience building consumer-facing interfaces at scale — most recently on transaction-critical systems including self-checkout, point-of-sale, and web POS platforms.

My work sits at the intersection of reliability and usability: feature-flag-driven rollouts, high-traffic consumer flows, and interfaces that have to work correctly the first time, every time. I care about the details that make a system feel invisible when it's working — and recoverable when it isn't.

Outside of shipping product, I spend time mentoring engineers and refining how teams ship UI safely at scale.
